Available Sources
Underlying Fund: the underlying fund that has been mapped to the asset will be used as the source of look through securities
Fund Position: the fund is also setup as a portfolio and, as a result, it can be used itself as the source of look through securities
Underlying Fund Benchmark: the benchmark of the underlying fund mapped to the asset will be used as the source of look through securities
Look Through Proxy: an alternative source for look through securities, configured through security attributes, will be used
