{{LABEL.OVERLAP}}
An averaging technique to mitigate the impact of error introduced by asynchronous market closing times. Defines the size of the overlapping time intervals. For example, a five day overlap is equivalent to taking the average return over five business days as the daily return, and then rolling the five day window forward for each subsequent observation.
