The table provides a summary of funds excluded from the Aladdin Commitment Risk Model projections.
There are two reasons why a fund is not covered:
  • The fund does not have capital account and risk information, as indicated by the following columns: "Contribution"; "Distribution"; "Portfolio Stand-Alone Risk"; and "Vintage Year." You can view these columns on the Risk & Exposure widget.
  • The fund's private asset class is not supported by the model, as indicated by the "APACS Asset Type" column.
A fund must have all of these attributes to be included within the model projections.
